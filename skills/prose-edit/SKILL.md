# Prose Edit

**Inputs:** Existing prose, audience/purpose, requested tone, and facts/structure to preserve.
**Output:** The edited text, with only consequential editorial notes when useful.
**Try:** “Use prose-edit to make this paragraph tighter and warmer without changing its meaning.” (use the skill name in your host's invocation syntax).

Honor the task scope and target-project policy; preserve unrelated work. Consult the [procedure](references/procedure.md) for operation details and the [shared contract](references/execution-contract.md) for authorization boundaries.

1. Preserve the author’s facts, intent, and useful voice. Tighten existing text; do not invent claims or expand scope into research or new content.
2. Use no em dashes. Limit colons and stacked clauses; split a sentence when that improves clarity.
3. Remove AI-isms, decorative metaphors, and “not X, but Y” or “not that, this” constructions. Prefer concrete verbs and direct statements.
4. Default to shorter without sacrificing clarity. “Warmer” means friendlier, not longer. Show the edited text; leave committing to the owner unless asked.

Report what actually happened, pending decisions, and the next action. Use target-project paths. [Provenance and pattern evidence](PROVENANCE.md).
